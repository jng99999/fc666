# Explicit requested-quantity Paper contract

`paper-requested-execution-v1` is an independent, deterministic economic contract in `core/paper/requested_execution.py`. It performs no I/O, persists no account changes and submits no orders. Existing closed-bar engines, preparation records and projected-funding formulas remain unchanged; production schema remains 0014.

A request freezes an explicit full quantity, protective limit, maximum fee rate, market rules, account base and stable content identity. The entire quantity must be funded before submission. BUY reserves quantity × limit plus a fee ceiling; SELL reserves inventory. Initial requests must meet minimum quantity/notional and price/quantity grids. Component partial fills may be smaller than the initial order minima, but must satisfy the grids, protective price, fee cap and cumulative requested quantity.

Events form a complete source prefix beginning at sequence zero. Receipt clocks cannot regress; execution clocks must fall between request creation and receipt. Identical deliveries are idempotent; conflicting identities, sequence collisions and gaps fail closed. Acknowledgement loss preserves uncertainty and funding. Unique fills alone change cash, inventory, cost basis, realized PnL and fees. BUY basis includes fees. SELL allocates basis proportionally with half-even rounding to 36 decimal places; disposing of all remaining inventory consumes the entire remaining basis.

Cancellation acknowledgement retains the remaining reservation. A later fill still settles against the original request. A cumulative receipt confirms current totals but does not close the source; a subsequent fill invalidates that confirmation. Only an explicit local `SEAL`, covering the preceding source sequence and matching cumulative totals after full fill, cancellation acknowledgement or rejection, releases remaining reservations. New facts after sealing require separate reconciliation and are rejected. A seal is a local simulation assertion, not authenticated exchange finality. Full fills have no unfilled quantity to reserve; separate fee adjustments are unsupported.

Asset quantities, prices and rates accept at most 18 decimal places and magnitude 1e18; monetary values accept at most 36 places and magnitude 1e40. Fee rates lie between zero and one. Arithmetic uses a 260-digit context; reservation fee ceilings round upward at 1e-36. Inputs must be finite decimal strings; realized PnL may be signed. Transcripts allow at most 1,000 delivered events and 32 MiB. IDs and clocks are bounded. These are versioned local contract limits, not venue capability claims.

`capture` exports the frozen request, transcript, reconstructed summary and content hash. Run `python -m scripts.verify_requested_execution FILE` to recompute and validate it offline. Hashes establish internal consistency, not origin authenticity; an attacker who rewrites and reseals all inputs can create another internally consistent report.

The isolated tests use explicit fixture balances and hand-calculated fills. No fixture enters product market data or account storage. PostgreSQL request journals, atomic durable fill acceptance, API/UI, concurrent shared capital, exchange transport and external reconciliation remain incomplete. The next integration must use a separate engine version and PostgreSQL transaction boundary while preserving prior ledgers and snapshots.
