# Fixed-allocation shared quote-capital forecast

`core.paper.shared_capital.evaluate(pool, journal_reports, candidate=None)` implements a deterministic offline quote-capital contract. It does not persist a shared pool or reserve shared capital. Definition fields are pool_id, quote_asset, opening_quote_cash, max_reserved_quote and canonical allocations; each allocation binds account_id/instrument_id/opening_quote_cash. Exact complete membership evidence is required, up to100 accounts and32MiB aggregate input/output.

Every complete journal export is independently replayed. Its opening identity/cash must equal the fixed allocation, with zero opening inventory, basis, fees and PnL. Allocations cannot exceed pool opening cash. Unallocated opening cash is counted once; account current cash is counted once. Account BUY holds remain part of account cash and are subtracted once to calculate available quote cash. Remaining inventory is not cash, equity, aggregate exposure or transferable funding. Later cash/PnL/fees come only from the supplied local journal replay. Initial funding and declared quote/instrument associations are configuration assertions, not verified bank or venue metadata.

Existing active holds are aggregated, including UNKNOWN outcomes and cancellation acknowledgements until explicit local sealing. Pool cap breaches are reported without modifying or releasing holds. Optional fresh candidate input freezes a fully funded local request against its own account, then forecasts existing pool holds plus its new BUY reservation against max_reserved_quote. It cannot borrow unallocated cash or another account's allocation. Exact Decimal arithmetic uses260-digit context and existing fee-ceiling rules. SELL shared inventory is explicitly unsupported even if the local request is funded.

Example: opening2500, two fixed allocations of1000 and unallocated500. One BUY hold404 yields current quote cash2500, reserved404, available2096. A second candidate with hold202 projects606; cap700 allows the capital forecast. A candidate hold404 projects808 and fails that cap. After the first account fills one unit at90 with0.90 fee, its cash909.10/hold303 and the pool cash2409.10/available2106.10 are replayed. Inventory valuation is outside this contract.

Every response keeps submission_allowed=false, shared_reservation_committed=false, coherent_capture_supported=false and exclusive_pool_membership_supported=false. A positive forecast is not risk authorization. Separate exports can be from different times, membership is not globally enforced and pools have no transactional write capability. Account controls, freshness, aggregate asset exposure, identity and scheduling gates are not implied.

`python -m scripts.verify_shared_capital report.json` verifies complete membership and forecast offline, rejects duplicate JSON keys and enforces32MiB bounds. Hashes do not authenticate input origin. Schema0021 and all existing engines, services, defaults and dependencies remain unchanged. No API, fixture-main data, private connector or Live capability is added.
