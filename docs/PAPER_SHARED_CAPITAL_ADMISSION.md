# Atomic pool-aware BUY preparation (head0023)

`shared_capital_admission.prepare(engine,pool_id,proposal,local_preview_sha256)` is an internal PostgreSQL capability. It locks the pool and all member accounts canonically, audits coherent current journals, rechecks the account's financial/control checkpoint, local risk/funding preview and current pool reservation cap, then commits the request/PREPARE gate/account hold and immutable shared admission receipt together. It does not expose HTTP shared preparation or submit to a venue.

The pool cap forecasts existing holds plus the full new BUY reservation. Per-account cash still funds the request; no borrowing, transfer or second economic ledger is introduced. Shared reservation means a mandatory pool admission attached atomically to the existing per-account hold, not a duplicate deduction of money. All supported new preparation paths reject pool members without this receipt. Existing exact retries may return original outcomes without another hold; the pool-aware path returns its original receipt even after later fills or unrelated pool activity. Conflicting proposal/digest retries fail.

Receipts contain the coherent pre-admission pool capture and independently replayed local risk preview. Mandatory admission records are checked on every pooled journal audit, bind to the current own-account financial/control prefix and immutable pool definition, and must cover every retained request. Missing declared admission fails closed without repair. Prior snapshots of other accounts remain server-captured immutable evidence; offline verification cannot authenticate physical locking, venue provenance or all other-account historical lineage.

Schema0023 adds immutable paper_capital_admissions with request/member/pool foreign keys. Since0022 constructors require financial revision0, supported pools cannot contain legacy pre-membership requests. Upgrade refuses existing pooled requests without admission rather than inventing retrospective evidence. Populated downgrade is blocked; empty roundtrip supported. Actual serialized admission history is limited32MiB/account before loading payloads, and rechecked after insert through journal audit; overflow rolls back. Individual receipts also have32MiB bounds.

Concurrent pooled preparations serialize through the pool row: with cap700, two404 holds cannot both commit. A rejection/fault leaves no request, gate, receipt or new hold. Existing source settlement/finalization may reduce holds under the account lock; capture/all-member locking observes a coherent state before another admission. These account-only operations never acquire a pool lock, avoiding reverse lock order. Fixed requests cannot increase their original BUY hold through supported settlement.

SELL shared inventory, transfer/reallocation, freshness/health, aggregate marked exposure and private transport remain unsupported. Unpooled accounts preserve their existing paths. No new operator tokens/grants or main fixtures. `python -m scripts.verify_shared_capital_admission receipt.json` replays evidence offline with input/duplicate-key bounds; unsigned receipt verification cannot authenticate commit/origin.
